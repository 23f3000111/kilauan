Questions, answered

It depends on the size of the piece and how dense the motifs are. A selayah or selendang can take from one to six months, so it helps to get in touch well before your date.

Yes. Every piece is made to order. You choose the form, the colour of the gauze and the motifs, and Norinda designs the layout with you before the first stitch.

The selayah is the smaller veil, about 55 × 95 cm, worn over the head and resting on the shoulders. The selendang is the wider shawl, about 77 × 156 cm, draped from the head to the waist.

Never wash or dry-clean it. Roll it around acid-free paper rather than folding it, keep it away from sunlight, moisture, perfume and cosmetics, and always handle it with clean, dry hands. Every piece comes with a care card.

Yes. Norinda teaches keringkam through classes and workshops, including National Training Week courses at UNIMAS. Choose "A place in a class" in the form above to ask about the next one.
