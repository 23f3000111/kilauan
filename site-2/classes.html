Classes and workshops

From your first threaded needle to a finished sampler, taught at the frame by Norinda K. Su'ut.

Ask about the next class

What you will learn

No experience needed. Every class starts from the basics and builds, stitch by stitch.

The materials

Sheer gauze, the flat gold strip and the needle that carries it.

The frame

Hemming the edges and stretching the gauze on the pedangan.

Counting threads

Placing each stitch by the weave, so motifs and borders sit true.

First motifs

Scattered stars first, then flowers, vines and the tali air border.

The right technique

Neat, even stitches. As Norinda tells her students: embroider with the right technique.

Caring for the work

How to roll, store and handle a finished piece so it lasts for generations.
